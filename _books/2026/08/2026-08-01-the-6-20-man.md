Amos Decker, John Puller, Will Robie and now, Travis Devine. They're all (mostly) the same guy. Baldacci has this archetype for a protagonist that he uses in all his series. He just changes one or two things about the guy and writes a few books before coming up with something a little different and doing it again. This was no different. Still, it was a thoroughly enjoyable read. Quick, summer read and I'll read the others because he writes books that are engaging and hard to put down once you start.
